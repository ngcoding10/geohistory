GeoHistory

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

GeoHistory is a free world history map. There are no reader accounts, nothing about you is collected unless you choose to send a suggestion, and the site currently shows no ads and uses no analytics.

Who this covers

This policy covers GeoHistory at ngcoding10.github.io/geohistory. "We" means Nico, the individual developer who runs it as a personal project.

What we collect

Nothing while you use the map. There is no sign-in for readers, and we never receive what you search for or which years you look at.

Suggestions you send

At the bottom of each panel you can open a form, "Suggest something we missed". Only if you press Send do we receive:

  • What you typed: the text, and the place, date and source link if you filled them in, plus the year and the state or event the panel was showing.
  • Your email, only if you chose to give one. It is used only to reply to you about the suggestion, never for anything else, and never shared.

We do not store your IP address. To stop spam, the server turns it into a scrambled code (a one-way hash with a secret salt) that changes every day and is used only to count how many suggestions arrive from one place; the counts delete themselves after a week. Suggestions are kept in our Google Firebase database (project in the United States), where only the site owner can read them, until they have been dealt with. To have a suggestion or your email removed, open an issue (below) or reply to our email.

What is stored in your browser

  • Preferences: globe or flat map, and which event types you have hidden. They are saved in your browser's local storage under names that start with geohistory., stay on your device, and are never sent anywhere.
  • The page address holds the year and place you are looking at (for example ?y=1776), so you can share or bookmark a view.

Clearing your browser's site data removes the preferences. No cookies are set.

Services your browser contacts

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and serves every file, including the map data. Like any web host, GitHub may log IP addresses.
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata are contacted only if you click a link to them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Google Firebase and reCAPTCHA are contacted only when you open the suggestion form: Firebase's code is loaded from Google, and reCAPTCHA checks that the request comes from this website and not from a script. Google's privacy policy applies to that check.

The map, its fonts and all history data are served from this site. No map tiles, fonts or scripts are loaded from other services while you use the map.

Ads and analytics

GeoHistory currently shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. If that changes, this page will be updated first, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Children

GeoHistory asks for no personal information. The email box in the suggestion form is optional; please leave it empty if you are under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/ngcoding10/geohistory/issues.
