History content may be wrong

  • Borders are snapshots for a few dozen years. For other years the map shows the nearest earlier snapshot, and the badge on the map says which year that is.
  • Borders before about 1500 are rough sketches, and later borders are simplified.
  • Events come from Wikidata, an open database anyone can edit. Dates, places and descriptions can be wrong or missing, and some regions and periods are covered much better than others.
  • Nothing in GeoHistory is an authoritative or official statement about history, borders or any country. Check anything important against a reliable source.
